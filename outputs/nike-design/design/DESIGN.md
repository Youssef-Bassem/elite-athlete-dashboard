# Design System: Nike-Inspired Athlete Recovery

## Visual Direction
- **Aesthetic**: Elite, intense, modern, athletic premium.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Primary: High-contrast Black (#000000) and White (#FFFFFF).
  - Accent: Subtle Green (#00FF00) strictly for positive recovery signals.
- **Typography**: Aggressive editorial typography. Large, bold headings with tight leading. Heavy sans-serif for impact.
- **UI Chrome**: Minimal. Thin borders, zero to minimal shadows, flat surfaces.
- **Hierarchy**: Strong visual weight on key performance indicators.

## Component Guidelines
- **Sidebar**: Minimalist left-aligned navigation.
- **Cards**: Compact, sharp edges, high contrast.
- **Visualizations**: Circular recovery scores, clean analytics charts.
- **Buttons**: Bold CTA hierarchy, high-contrast states.

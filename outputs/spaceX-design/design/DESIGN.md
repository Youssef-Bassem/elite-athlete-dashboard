# SpaceX-Inspired Design System

## Visual Identity
- **Aesthetic**: Ultra-minimal engineering, precision-driven, aerospace-grade.
- **Palette**: Monochrome (Deep Blacks #000000, Dark Grays #121212, Crisp Whites #FFFFFF).
- **Typography**: Restrained sans-serif, technical monospaced accents. High contrast, small font sizes for secondary data.
- **Hierarchy**: Data-first, extreme whitespace discipline, thin dividers (0.5pt - 1pt) instead of shadows.
- **Atmosphere**: Futuristic, calm, mission-critical.

## Components
- **Layout**: Rigid grid, modular analytics cards.
- **Indicators**: Subtle status lights (dimmed vs. active), thin progress bars.
- **Navigation**: Collapsed or minimal sidebar with icon-only or short labels.
